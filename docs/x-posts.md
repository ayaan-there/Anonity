## Tweet 2 — Technical insight

The neat ZK trick in Anonity:

When a bounty poster resolves a submission, the circuit proves "caller holds the secret that produced THIS bounty's org commitment" — inside the proof.

The chain verifies authority. It never learns which wallet posted the bounty.

Same pattern lets hunters prove "I'm owed this payout" while staying unlinkable across every bounty they've ever touched.

Identity = persistentHash(salt ‖ round ‖ secret). Keys never leave your machine.
